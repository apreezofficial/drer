Your voice assistant

Say “Buddy, open Mail”, or “Buddy”, wait for “Hmm?”, then speak your request. “Hey” is optional. English speech recognition runs on your Mac.

Enable voice in the sidebar to grant microphone and Speech Recognition access. It stays active while the window is hidden; turn it off there or quit DRER to stop listening. Once enabled, voice resumes when you reopen DRER. Audio is not sent to Groq; commands needing AI send only their text.
